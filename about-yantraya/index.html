About Yantraya Solutions

An engineering, technical training and industrial consultancy organisation based in Vadodara, Gujarat, bringing engineering education, practical skill development, workforce capability building and manufacturing process improvement under one roof.

The problem we solve

Engineering graduates often know the theory but not the workplace. Manufacturers struggle to find and develop capable people, and many SMEs make good products without the documentation and process control their customers expect.

Yantraya works on all three problems. Our Engineering Academy & Project Lab makes engineering education practical. Workforce Solutions turns technical knowledge into workplace competence. SME Audit & Compliance helps manufacturers see and close the gaps in their systems.

We work with

  • Engineering students and graduates
  • Engineering colleges and universities
  • Schools and educational institutions, through appropriate technical programmes
  • Manufacturing and engineering companies
  • MSMEs and SMEs
  • HR, quality, production and engineering departments
  • Organisations improving employee competencies and operational systems

Our purpose

To bridge the gap between engineering knowledge, practical application and industrial expectations.
