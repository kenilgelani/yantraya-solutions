How every session runs

The same four parts each time, so you move from watching to doing the work yourself.

  1. Explain

    The concept, in clear language.

  2. Demonstrate

    How it works, on a real example.

  3. Practise

    You do the task independently.

  4. Review

    We check the work and explain mistakes.

Your journey from enquiry to completion

  1. Enquiry

    We learn your course, semester, subject, project requirement and goal.

  2. Initial assessment

    We find your current knowledge, practical skills and learning gaps.

  3. Recommended plan

    A suitable course or package, with outcomes, schedule and fee.

  4. Registration

    Payment terms, policies, timetable and the tools you need.

  5. Learning and practice

    Small steps, exercises, checks of understanding and feedback.

  6. Progress review

  7. Final assessment

    Practical ability and understanding, not just attendance.

  8. Completion

    A progress summary, a completion certificate where appropriate, and next steps.
