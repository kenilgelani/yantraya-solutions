Mechanical SME Audit & Compliance

We help small and medium manufacturers find the gaps in their quality systems, documents and shop-floor practices, and close them before the customer’s auditor arrives.

Five questions we help you answer

Many small manufacturers make good products but struggle to keep records, control drawings and investigate defects consistently.

  • Are we following our documented processes?
  • Are our quality records complete and easy to retrieve?
  • Can we identify and control defective products?
  • Do we investigate problems and verify corrective actions?
  • Can we show our processes and records during a customer or management-system audit?

What we look at

The scope is agreed with you before any visit. These are the areas most assessments draw from.

Document and drawing control

Approved drawings, current revisions at the workstation, authorised changes and control of obsolete copies.

Incoming material and traceability

Identification, purchase and inspection records, and batch, heat or lot traceability through production.

In-process and final inspection

Defined criteria, correct drawings, clear responsibility, recorded results and control of rejected parts.

Measuring equipment and calibration records

Equipment identification, calibration status and control of overdue or damaged equipment.

Nonconforming product and corrective action

Segregation, disposition authority, root-cause investigation and effectiveness checks.

Supplier quality and customer requirements

Supplier selection and monitoring, incoming requirements and follow-up of supplier corrective actions.

How an engagement runs

  1. Enquiry

    Your company, industry, main problem and reason for seeking support.

  2. Discovery

    Current process, customer requirements, recent findings and deadline.

  3. Scope and criteria

    What will be reviewed, against which requirements, and what is excluded.

  4. Quotation

    Fee, schedule, confidentiality and responsibilities, confirmed in writing.

  5. Preparation

    Document review and a checklist tailored to the agreed scope.

  6. Assessment

    On site or remote: observation, interviews and record sampling.

  7. Findings

    The criterion, the objective evidence, the gap and its practical significance.

  8. Report and closing meeting

    Findings discussed, facts clarified and actions agreed.

  9. Corrective-action support

    Root causes, owners, deadlines and the evidence needed.

  10. Follow-up review

    Evidence checked; completed actions separated from open ones.

Evidence, not assumptions

Every finding names the criterion, the evidence reviewed and the gap observed. A report never says only that “documentation is poor”.

  • Ask
  • Observe
  • Check
  • Compare
  • Record
  • Confirm

Fixing a problem so it stays fixed

1. Describe the problem
What happened, where, when and what evidence exists.
2. Find the root cause
5 Whys or cause-and-effect analysis, from evidence.
3. Decide the action
What changes, who owns it and by when.
4. Keep evidence
Updated instructions, training records or inspection results.
5. Check effectiveness
Later evidence shows whether the problem has stopped.

Readiness support, not certification

We help you prepare for audits and improve your systems. Certification decisions for ISO or IATF belong to a certification body, and statutory inspections belong to authorised inspectors.

We do not promise that you will pass an audit, and we never create or backdate records. Your drawings, inspection reports and customer requirements stay confidential.
