Engineering Subjects Academy

Understand engineering concepts. Apply them to real engineering problems.

The problem

Many students can reproduce an answer but cannot apply the concept to a new numerical or a real component. A gap in one subject then carries into design, projects and interviews.

Who it is for

  • Diploma and undergraduate engineering students
  • Mechanical, automobile, production and allied branches
  • Postgraduate students revisiting fundamentals
  • Students preparing for semester examinations

What we provide

  • Mechanical engineering core subjects
  • Manufacturing Processes
  • Engineering Mechanics
  • Strength of Materials
  • Theory of Machines
  • Machine Design
  • Engineering Drawing
  • Production and Industrial Engineering
  • Manufacturing Technology
  • Quality Engineering
  • Automation in Manufacturing
  • Subject-specific academic support and examination preparation

Delivery formats

  • Individual subject classes
  • Small-group batches
  • Online and offline sessions
  • Concept-based workshops
  • Problem-solving and numerical sessions

How we work

  1. Enquiry

    Your course, semester, subject, project requirement and goal.

  2. Initial assessment

    Your current knowledge, practical skills and learning gaps.

  3. Recommended plan

    A course or support package with outcomes, schedule and fee.

  4. Learning and practice

    Small steps, exercises, checks of understanding and feedback.

  5. Assessment

    Practical ability and understanding, not just attendance.

  6. Next steps

    A progress summary and what to practise next.

What you receive

Depending on the agreed scope.

  • A short diagnostic of your current understanding
  • A topic plan agreed before classes start
  • Worked examples and practice questions
  • A check of understanding at the end of each topic
  • Revision recommendations

Questions about this service

Who can enrol in Yantraya Engineering Academy?

Diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students, mainly from mechanical, automobile, production and allied branches, as well as research scholars. Engineering colleges and faculty members can also engage us for programmes. Tell us your course and semester and we will suggest where to start.

Do you provide online and offline engineering training?

Yes. Depending on the course, sessions run online or in person in Vadodara, individually or in small batches. Hands-on CAD sessions need computers with the right software, so the format is confirmed when you enrol.

Are course completion certificates provided?

Where appropriate, yes: a certificate of completion from Yantraya after the final assessment. It is not a university degree or a statutory qualification.
