Privacy policy

How Yantraya Solutions handles the information you share with us. Last updated November 2026.

What this website collects

This website does not store the information you type into its enquiry forms. When you send an enquiry, the form opens WhatsApp or your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

What we receive

If you send an enquiry, call or message us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email, organisation and the details of your requirement.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal you asked for and deliver services you engage us for. We do not sell your information.

Project and client information

Student work, research files, drawings, inspection records and other client information are kept confidential and used only for the agreed work. We publish case studies, photographs or testimonials only with permission.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows a Google Maps map on the contact page. Those services may receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. If analytics is enabled, it measures visits and enquiry clicks in aggregate.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Contact us at yantrayasolutions@zohomail.in or +91 97252 04739.
