About Yantraya Solutions

An engineering, technical training and industrial consultancy organisation in Vadodara, Gujarat, bringing education, skill development, workforce capability and manufacturing improvement under one roof.

The problem we solve

Engineering graduates often know the theory but not the workplace. Manufacturers struggle to develop capable people, and many SMEs make good products without the documentation and process control their customers expect.

Our Academy makes engineering education practical. Workforce Solutions turns knowledge into workplace competence. SME Audit & Compliance helps manufacturers see and close the gaps in their systems.

  • Engineering students and graduates
  • Engineering colleges and universities
  • Schools and institutions, through technical programmes
  • Manufacturing and engineering companies
  • MSMEs and SMEs
  • HR, quality, production and engineering departments
our purpose

To bridge the gap between engineering knowledge, practical application and industrial expectations.

our vision

To become a trusted engineering and industrial development partner that builds capable professionals, supports innovation and strengthens manufacturing businesses.

Our mission

  • Make engineering education more practical and application-oriented.
  • Develop industry-ready engineering professionals.
  • Support research, innovation and product development.
  • Improve technical competencies within manufacturing organisations.
  • Help SMEs strengthen quality, documentation and operational systems.
