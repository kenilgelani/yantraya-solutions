How we work

  1. Enquiry

    Your course, semester, subject, project requirement and goal.

  2. Initial assessment

    Your current knowledge, practical skills and learning gaps.

  3. Recommended plan

    A course or support package with outcomes, schedule and fee.

  4. Learning and practice

    Small steps, exercises, checks of understanding and feedback.

  5. Assessment

    Practical ability and understanding, not just attendance.

  6. Next steps

    A progress summary and what to practise next.

What you receive

  • A feasibility review before full commitment
  • A written scope and milestone plan
  • Design and calculation reviews
  • Vendor quotations for prototype work, for your approval
  • Documentation and presentation feedback

Your project stays yours

You do and understand the work; we guide, review and give feedback. We do not sell completed projects for submission.

3D printing, machining, fabrication and testing are carried out through approved vendors and charged separately, only after you approve the quotation.

Questions about this service

Do you provide final-year project guidance?

Yes, for diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate projects. We usually start with a feasibility review, then agree a written scope and milestones. You do the work and remain the owner of your project; we guide, review and give feedback.

Do you support prototype development?

Yes, through planning, design review and coordination of 3D printing, machining and fabrication with approved vendors. Vendor costs are quoted to you and approved before any order is placed.
