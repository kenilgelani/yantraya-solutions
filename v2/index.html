Yantraya Solutions is an engineering, technical training and industrial consultancy organisation based in Vadodara, Gujarat. One object runs through all our work: the engineering drawing. A student learns to read it, a fresher learns to work from it, and an auditor checks that it is the current revision.

Our purpose is to bridge the gap between engineering knowledge, practical application and what industry actually expects.

Our approach

The same five steps for a student, an HR manager or a factory owner.

every engagement, every time

  1. Understand

    Your goal, your people or your process, before anything is proposed.

  2. Design

    A programme, plan or assessment scoped to what you need.

  3. Deliver

    Practical teaching, training or review, done to the agreed scope.

  4. Evaluate

    Assessments and evidence show what changed.

  5. Improve

    Recommendations for the next step, not just a certificate.
