Privacy policy

How Yantraya Solutions handles the information you share with us. Last updated November 2026.

What this website collects

This website does not store what you type into its enquiry forms. Sending an enquiry opens WhatsApp or your own email app with your message filled in; nothing is sent until you press send there.

What we receive

If you send an enquiry, call or message us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email, organisation and requirement.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal you asked for and deliver services you engage us for. We do not sell your information.

Project and client information

Student work, research files, drawings and inspection records stay confidential and are used only for the agreed work. Case studies, photographs and testimonials are published only with permission.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows a Google Maps map on the contact page. Those services may receive your IP address under their own policies.
