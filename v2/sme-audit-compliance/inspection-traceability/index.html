Inspection, Measurement & Traceability Assessment

Improve the reliability and consistency of inspection and measurement practices.

Enquire about this serviceWhatsApp us

The problem

If inspection is inconsistent or material cannot be traced, one customer complaint can mean sorting or recalling far more than the faulty parts.

Who it is for

  • Quality and inspection teams
  • Suppliers with traceability requirements
  • Companies with recurring inspection escapes

What we provide

  • Incoming inspection process review
  • In-process inspection review
  • Final inspection process review
  • Measurement instrument management assessment
  • Calibration record review
  • Inspection report formats
  • Product identification
  • Material traceability
  • Inspection acceptance criteria review
  • Quality record management

How we work

  1. Initial consultation

    The company, its processes, customer expectations and concerns.

  2. Assessment & evidence

    Documents, records, processes and workplace practices reviewed.

  3. Gap analysis

    Nonconformities, weaknesses and opportunities against the agreed criteria.

  4. Action plan

    Findings prioritised, responsibilities and timelines recommended.

  5. Implementation support

    Documentation, corrective actions and process improvements.

  6. Follow-up & review

    Action closure checked and improvement evaluated.

What you receive

  • Inspection gap assessment
  • Measurement process recommendations
  • Inspection format suggestions
  • Traceability improvement recommendations

We review calibration records; we do not calibrate

Calibration itself must be carried out by a suitably equipped and, where required, accredited laboratory.

Questions about this service

Do you provide on-site assessments?

Yes. Most assessments include a site visit, agreed in advance and carried out under your safety, PPE and confidentiality rules. Some document reviews can be done remotely.
