FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can enrol in Yantraya Engineering Academy?

Diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students, mainly from mechanical, automobile, production and allied branches, as well as research scholars. Engineering colleges and faculty members can also engage us for programmes. Tell us your course and semester and we will suggest where to start.

Do you provide online and offline engineering training?

Yes. Depending on the course, sessions run online or in person in Vadodara, individually or in small batches. Hands-on CAD sessions need computers with the right software, so the format is confirmed when you enrol.

Which CAD/CAM software do you cover?

Creo Parametric, Siemens NX, SolidWorks and CATIA for CAD, along with CNC programming fundamentals and CAM toolpath generation. We teach only with legally licensed software; ask which tools are running in the current batch.

Do you provide final-year project guidance?

Yes, for diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate projects. We usually start with a feasibility review, then agree a written scope and milestones. You do the work and remain the owner of your project; we guide, review and give feedback.

Do you support prototype development?

Yes, through planning, design review and coordination of 3D printing, machining and fabrication with approved vendors. Vendor costs are quoted to you and approved before any order is placed.

Do you provide research and thesis mentoring?

Yes, within the subject areas we can competently support. We help with research questions, literature-search planning, methodology, analysis and thesis structure. We do not write theses for submission, invent data or guarantee publication.

Can engineering colleges partner with Yantraya?

Yes. Colleges can engage us for technical workshops, CAD/CAM programmes, project guidance for student groups and faculty development. Choose the institutional option in the enquiry form.

Are course completion certificates provided?

Where appropriate, yes: a certificate of completion from Yantraya after the final assessment. It is not a university degree or a statutory qualification.
