Train-the-Trainer (TTT) Programme

Develop internal trainers who can deliver technical training consistently within your organisation.

Enquire about this service WhatsApp

The problem

Experienced employees know the work but often struggle to teach it, so how well a new joiner learns depends on who happens to train them.

Who it is for

  • Manufacturing companies (primary)
  • Engineering colleges and technical institutions (secondary)

What we provide

  • Trainer identification
  • Technical subject preparation
  • Training delivery skills
  • Demonstration techniques
  • Adult learning principles
  • Presentation and communication
  • Training material development
  • Practical session management
  • Trainee assessment
  • Training effectiveness measurement
  • Internal training system development

Programme structure

3Training days
18Total hours
6–8Participants

Followed by a 30-day workplace review of how the training is being applied, with support for implementation.

Learning methodology

  1. Learn

    The subject and how adults learn

  2. Demonstrate

    Show the task correctly

  3. Practise

    Rehearse delivery

  4. Teach

    Run a real session

  5. Assess

    Get assessed and feedback

How we work

    The company's workforce and business requirements.

  2. Assess

    Existing employee competencies.

  3. Design

    Customised training plans and modules.

  4. Train

    Technical and practical training.

  5. Evaluate

    Learning and skill improvement.

  6. Improve

What you receive

Depending on the agreed scope.

  • Internal trainers who have practised and been assessed
  • Training materials for your own subjects
  • Trainee assessment checklists
  • A 30-day workplace follow-up review

Questions about this service

What is the Train-the-Trainer programme?

A three-day, 18-hour programme for 6 to 8 participants that turns experienced employees into internal trainers. It follows Learn, Demonstrate, Practise, Teach and Assess, with a 30-day workplace follow-up.

How do you measure training effectiveness?

We compare baseline and final assessments on comparable tasks, assess practical skills, collect participant and supervisor feedback and, where agreed, check later whether the skills are being applied at work. Attendance alone is not treated as success.
