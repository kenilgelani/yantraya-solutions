Workforce Solutions

We help engineering talent become workplace-ready through practical training, skill assessment and structured development programmes built around industry requirements.

We don’t sell training hours

We identify skill gaps, train people against specific requirements, measure improvement and recommend the next step.

  1. Understand the skill gap

    What the role needs against what people can do today.

  2. Train against the requirement

    Content built for the specific employees and skills involved.

  3. Assess the result

    Baseline and final assessments on comparable tasks.

  4. Improve the programme

    Feedback from learners and employers shapes the next run.

How a training engagement runs

  1. Enquiry and discovery

    The customer, learner group, job roles and problem to solve.

  2. Training needs analysis

    The gap between current and expected skills.

  3. Proposal and scope

    Objectives, modules, duration, learners, venue, fees and exclusions.

  4. Preparation

    Content, exercises, trainers, software, equipment and assessments.

  5. Delivery

    Sessions run to plan, with attendance and participation recorded.

  6. Assessment

    The skills the programme was designed to teach are evaluated.

  7. Reporting

    A concise report on what was delivered and demonstrated.

  8. Follow-up

    Where agreed, a later check that skills are being applied.

What your report contains

A well-attended course is not necessarily an effective one, so we report what learners can actually do.

  • Training delivered, attendance and completion
  • Baseline and final assessment results
  • Competencies demonstrated
  • Skills needing further development
  • Participant feedback
  • Recommended next steps

What we promise, and what we don’t

We teach, assess and report on skills within our competence. Specialist trainers are brought in for subjects that need them.

We are not a recruitment or staffing agency and we do not guarantee placement. Certificates are certificates of completion from Yantraya, not statutory or regulated credentials.
